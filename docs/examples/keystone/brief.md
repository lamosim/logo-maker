# Keystone: brief

## The business
Keystone sends invoices that get paid faster, for solo consultants, solving late payments.

## The one idea
The piece that holds everything together.

## Only statement
We are the only invoicing tool that nags clients so you don't have to, for solo consultants who would rather do the work than chase the money.

## Personality
Adjectives: steady (not boring), direct (not rude), warm (not cute)
Persona: a calm site foreman who is good at follow-up

## The name
Written: Keystone (capital K, one word); said: KEE-stone, two syllables, stress on the first; story: the stone that locks an arch; tagline: none

## Industry tag
invoicing for solo consultants, which selects the fintech cliché list

## What I assumed
- Archetype: the persona leads. A calm foreman is plain-spoken and sturdy (Everyman); being good at follow-up is looking after people (Caregiver).
- Aaker scores: warm and direct lift sincerity to 0.9; steady and the persona's follow-up lift competence to 0.8, and Stripe's restraint nudges it to 0.9; the calm persona keeps excitement under neutral, and "not boring" floors it at 0.4; the plain-spoken foreman keeps sophistication at 0.4; Patagonia's conviction nudges ruggedness to 0.6, and "not rude" caps it there.
- Industry: the tag names what Keystone sells (invoicing), not who buys it, so the audit checks the fintech list; "consultants" does not select the consulting list.
- Pronunciation: the usual English KEE-stone; you gave only the written form.
- Ember orange: a warm red-orange around a glowing coal, not a traffic-cone orange; the exact colour is chosen in territories and refinement.
- Harshest: embroidery means the mark must hold in one colour, with no hairlines and no gradients; the 16 px favicon means a redrawn favicon tier.
- The arch story is available to the mark but not required of it: the strategy decides whether the mark draws an arch, a K or neither.
