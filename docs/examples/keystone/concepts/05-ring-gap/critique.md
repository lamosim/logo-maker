# 05-ring-gap: critique

Idea: a heavy ring with a gap at the top that only its keystone closes: the loop holds once the last piece is in. This is the fifth direction, "a ring with a gap" (controller ruling P33), and it stays among the survivors for the owner.

## Round 1

Ring of outer radius 400 and band 150; the keystone is the 30-degree sector at the top, set off by two radial joints 30 units wide. Small tier: band 175, joints 70. Favicon: the keystone dropped and the 30-degree gap left open. Audit ok, no warnings; thinnest feature 9.38 px at 64 px, 3.56 px at 32 px, 3.59 px at 16 px.

The critique sheet caught what the audit cannot: at 48 to 64 px the ring with one separated segment at the top looks like a loading spinner or a donut chart, and the open favicon (32 to 16 px) reads as a power button. At 256 px the idea reads, a ring of stone with its keystone.

## Round 2

The favicon now keeps the keystone and closes its joints (a whole ring), and the small tier's joints narrow from 70 to 62 units (still open at 32 px). Audit ok, no warnings; favicon thinnest feature 3.53 px at 16 px.

The sheet: the power-button reading at 16 to 32 px is gone, replaced by a plain closed ring, which is generic but neutral. The small tier (48 to 64 px), the size the concept sheet's app-icon and avatar mockups draw, still shows a ring with a separated top segment, the spinner reading. That is inherent in a ring with a gap: narrowing the joints further would close them at 32 px and fail the one-colour check. Universal and distinctive stay low; this risk is said to the owner when the sheet is shown.

Audit warnings: none to fix or justify.

## Scores

appropriate 4, distinctive 2, simple 5, legible 4, adaptable 3, timeless 4, universal 2, clear 3, congruent 3, system 4: 34 of 50. It passes the gate (appropriate, simple and legible at 4 or more), so it may be shown; its two scores of 2 (distinctive, universal) go to the owner with their reasons.

Look-alike search (7 Oct 2026): segmented rings and pie marks are everywhere in stock libraries. These checks are a knock-out only, not legal clearance: they can surface an obvious conflict but cannot prove there is none, so have a trademark attorney run a clearance search before you file the name or spend money on it.

## Rationale draft

Keystone's mark is a ring of stone that only closes when its last piece drops in. An invoice is the same loop: work done, bill sent, money in, and Keystone keeps after the last part until the circle closes.
