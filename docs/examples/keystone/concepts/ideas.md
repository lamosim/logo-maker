# Keystone: ideas

The one idea: the piece that holds everything together. Territory: Hearth Stone (Bricolage Grotesque 700, ember on coal and kiln paper, heavy strokes, softened corners). Black only in this phase.

Each line is one idea, tagged with its motifs. Struck lines use a motif on the fintech or Any blacklist (cliches.md) or the owner's off-limits list, and the brief does not make it the strategy.

1. A K that holds a stone in its jaw: the keystone cut from the letter's own counter. [letter K, keystone, wedge]
2. A heavy round arch on its own feet whose keystone rises above the crown. [arch, keystone]
3. Two stacked blocks locked together by one keystone-shaped dovetail. [stacked blocks, dovetail, keystone]
4. The wordmark with one quirk: the counter of the o cut as an arch. [wordmark, arch counter]
5. A ring with a gap at the top, closed by its keystone: the loop that only holds once the last piece drops in. [ring, keystone]
6. The K's junction drawn as a keystone, so the arm and the leg lean on it like the two halves of an arch on its side. [letter K, keystone]
7. A flat (jack) arch of wedge stones over an opening, the central wedge the key. [flat arch, wedge stones]
8. A keystone alone, a heavy trapezoid with softened corners. [keystone] (too close to a flower pot or a bin when it stands alone; kept only as a part of other ideas)
9. A cairn of blocks crowned by a keystone. [stacked blocks, cairn] (too many parts for 16 px, and a stepped pile reads as a growth pyramid)
10. A stone wall in running bond with one wedge block holding the courses. [brick wall, wedge] (a brick wall is the firewall icon)
11. A bridge arch spanning two banks: work on one side, payment on the other. [bridge, arch]
12. A foreman's level, the bubble centred: steady, checked, done. [spirit level]
13. A clasp that closes two halves. [clasp]
14. ~~An upward arrow built from stacked stones.~~ [upward arrow] Struck: the owner's off-limits list and the fintech blacklist.
15. ~~A shield with a keystone at its top.~~ [shield] Struck: off limits, and on the fintech list.
16. ~~A coin with a keystone-shaped slot.~~ [coin] Struck: fintech blacklist (coin, piggy bank).
17. ~~An arch on two columns: a temple front.~~ [column, pillar] Struck: fintech blacklist; it is the bank façade.
18. ~~Two hands holding a stone.~~ [handshake, hands] Struck: fintech blacklist (handshake).
19. A bell that rings until the invoice is paid. [bell] (the generic notification icon of every app; dropped)
20. A plumb bob hanging true. [plumb bob] (tools read as trades, not money)

Directions taken forward (five; at least three structurally different):

- 01-k-jaw (lettermark, built from the glyph): idea 1.
- 02-arch (pictorial, from primitives): idea 2.
- 03-dovetail (abstract, a tile cut by one joint): idea 3.
- 04-wordmark (wordmark, type with one parametric quirk): idea 4.
- 05-ring-gap (a ring with a gap and its keystone, from the ring primitive): idea 5.

Ideas 6, 7 and 11 are variations of 1 and 2; 8, 9 and 10 fail the 16 px favicon or carry an unintended reading; 12, 13, 19 and 20 do not carry the one idea as directly as a stone does.
