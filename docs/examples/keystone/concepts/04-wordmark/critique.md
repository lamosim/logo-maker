# 04-wordmark: critique

Idea: the name in Bricolage Grotesque 700 with one quirk: the counter of the o is cut as a round arch with a flat sill. The compact, small and favicon tiers draw the o alone (kit-api.md's fourth example pattern).

## Round 1

Audit ok, no warnings: full-wordmark x-height 15.5 px at 120 px; the o icon's thinnest feature 8.56 px at 32 px and 4.66 px at 16 px.

The critique sheet: at 256 px the arched counter is visible and pleasant, a doorway in the o; at 128 px it is gone. The o icon (64 to 16 px) is a heavy ring with a flat-bottomed hole, and at 16 px a plain o or zero. Because the concept has no symbol, every icon from 96 px up (the iOS app icon, apple-touch-icon, icon-512, the Android launchers) would show the whole wordmark (logo-types.md, deliverables.md), and the app icon is the brief's first surface.

Before a second round I tried the obvious alternative quirk in a scratch render: a keystone-shaped crossbar on the t, the middle letter of the word. It read as a torch or a funnel, never as a keystone, so no quirk tried here carries the one idea.

## Verdict

Killed after round 1 (scorecard.json keeps all ten scores). The direction's limits are structural, not a matter of proportion: the arch quirk carries the arch, not the piece that holds it, and a wordmark-only mark cannot be the app icon. Giving it a symbol would turn it into a combination mark that repeats 01 or 02, which is a blend, not a revision.

Audit warnings: none.
