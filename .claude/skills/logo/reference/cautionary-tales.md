# Cautionary tales

Read this when: writing the category audit and the strategy, sorting a negative reaction into unfamiliarity or a real flaw, doing the unintended-readings pass in refinement, and before writing any rationale.

Each case is a rule with a price attached. Most are rebrands; for a new venture they set the rules for the first mark, so that it never needs one of these rescues [05 §2] [01 §2].

## Cases

| Case | What happened | What went wrong | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kia, 2021 | Angular joined letters; 26% of respondents read "KN"; about 30,000 monthly searches for "KN car" | stylisation beat legibility; a join erased a letter | a wordmark must pass the first-read test with strangers |
| University of California, Dec 2012 | A "C inside a U" read as a flushing toilet or a loading icon; 30,000+ petition signatures; suspended within a week | an unintended reading, and a start-up register for an institution that values tradition | run an unintended-readings pass: rotate, mirror, squint, animate |
| Pepsi, 2008 | A 27-page "Breathtaking" document justified the tilted smile with the golden ratio, the Parthenon and "the Pepsi ratio" | post-rationalised mysticism; the document became the joke | construction diagrams are tools, not rationales; if the story is bigger than the mark, the mark is weak |
| Verizon 2024, Kenvue 2023 | "Veritas and Horizon"; a rectangle "representing scientific precision" | etymological and geometric rationales rarely reach the public [01 §2] | do not over-explain |
| Meta, Oct 2021 | An infinity-loop M; LogoLounge counted 1,200+ infinity logos, and a near-identical 2015 mark surfaced within days [01 §2] | a famous-but-shared shape, no look-alike search | knock out look-alikes before presenting |
| Jaguar, Nov 2024 | "Copy Nothing": a film with no cars, a new wordmark, the leaper reduced; European sales down 97.5% year on year in April 2025, confounded by a planned model pause | showed attitude before product; dropped heritage assets; no bridge for existing customers | never launch an identity ahead of the thing it identifies |
| Cracker Barrel, Aug 2025 | Dropped "Uncle Herschel" for a text-only logo; shares fell 10 to 15 percent, roughly $100M or more of value [01 §2]; reversed within a week | "decades of accumulated meaning" deleted for digital tidiness | score assets for fame and uniqueness before deleting; modernise a character's execution, not its existence |
| Twitter to X, Jul 2023 | The bird and the word "tweet" deleted overnight for a fan-designed X; $4 to 20B of brand value estimated lost | 15+ years of equity erased; a generic letter cannot be owned | a generic glyph cannot be uniquely owned |
| HBO Max to Max to HBO Max, 2023 to 2025 | Renamed "Max", in a blue chosen as "the most liked color, universally"; subscribers kept saying HBO Max; reversed May 2025 | dropped the one word carrying the quality | test recognition, not likability; "when your customers tell you who you are... believe them" |
| Gap, Oct 2010 | Helvetica and a small gradient square, "clip art chic"; reverted in six days | a distinctive asset swapped for a generic sans; no testing | a logo change cannot substitute for strategy |
| Tropicana, Jan 2009 | The orange-with-straw removed; sales fell about 20% in two months; reverted within a month | shoppers "didn't recognize the product on supermarket shelves" | recognition at a glance beats elegance |
| Luxury "blanding", c. 2018 | Burberry, Saint Laurent, Balenciaga and Balmain converged on bold all-caps sans; Burberry restored its 1901 knight in 2023 | "homogeneity is dangerous": famous but not unique | a category uniform is the opposite of a distinctive asset |
| Johnson & Johnson, Sept 2023 | The 1887 script replaced by a sans; "may have just thrown away its most valuable visual asset" | a unique asset traded for a category-standard sans | solve digital legibility with a short-form mark, never by killing the hero asset |
| London 2012, 2007 | A jagged "2012", a 48,000-signature petition; organisers held and the system defined the Games; one animation drew epilepsy complaints | deliberately dissonant; accessibility untested | provocation can work if the system delivers and the client holds its nerve; test accessibility |
| Airbnb Bélo, Jul 2014 | Mocked for anatomical readings and look-alikes; the brand held; the mark is now unremarkable | over-explained meaning ("belong anywhere"); no readings pass | expect day-one backlash on an abstract mark; "the test is month twelve, provided the mark is sound" |
| Instagram, May 2016 | Camera icon replaced by a gradient glyph; outrage, then acceptance | nothing structural: it kept the camera silhouette and the rainbow cue | keep the silhouette and the colour cue; people hate change for about a quarter |
| Mastercard, Jan 2019 | Dropped its name once "more than 80 percent" recognised the circles alone | not a failure: the benchmark | symbol-only requires measured, majority unaided recognition |

All rows from 05 §2 unless marked [01 §2]; 01 §2 adds the recoveries: Burberry's restored knight, PayPal escaping "the blue that has become synonymous with fintech", Walmart's wordmark from Sam Walton's hat. The most-praised moves of the decade were retrievals, not inventions [01 §3 rule 4].

## Rules

The six generalisable rules [05 §2], and what each means for a new venture:

1. Inventory and score existing assets before designing. A new venture may already own something: a holding page, a colour on the pitch deck, a shipped app icon. Ask about it in intake (questionnaire.md, question 6) and score it for fame and uniqueness (strategy-frameworks.md) [05 §3.3].
2. The mark should explain itself in a glance, not in a 27-page document. Write one paragraph for the owner, never "represents", never a construction diagram, ratio or golden ratio [spec §3.3] [05 §5.3].
3. Run the strangers-read, unintended-readings and accessibility tests. Read the critique sheet's mirrored and rotated panels; turn the 512 px panel upside down in your mind for 180 degrees; squint at the 16 and 24 px panels; read the refine sheet's colour-vision strip. Record findings in `final/knockout.md` [spec §4.6] [05 §5.4].
4. Keep silhouette or colour continuity when evolving. For a first mark, build a silhouette and one owned colour that can survive the first redesign [02 §K rule 17].
5. Derive a short form rather than deleting the hero asset. Draw the `small` and `favicon` tiers from the mark's own DNA instead of inventing a second mark [05 §4.3].
6. Plan for change-aversion, but tell it apart from real equity loss (Cracker Barrel) and from a legibility failure (Kia). The probing question in process.md does the sorting [05 §2].
